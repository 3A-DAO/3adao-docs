Borrowing EURO3 from a Vault triggers a **one-off issuance fee** that is **charged in EURO3** on the drawn amount and added to the total debt.

The Issuance fee is set **between 0.5% and 5%** depending on the overall collateral risk characteristics and the demand and supply of EURO3.

The initial origination fees per whitelisted tokens are set by the Risk Model and listed [here](/3a-protocol/whitelisting-criteria/whitelisted-tokens).
