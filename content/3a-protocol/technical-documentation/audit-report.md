<!-- archive-notice -->
> **Archived.** The 3A lending protocol and EURO3 on Linea and Polygon were phased out in 2024: no new loans from
> 1 October 2024, and all remaining vaults closed on 30 November 2024
> ([3A DAO's announcement](https://medium.com/@3aDAO/phasing-out-euro3-lending-f5869a3498a8)). This page is kept
> for reference and describes the protocol as it was. Do not send funds to the contracts it lists.

> **Tip:** Link to FYEO Audit Report [here](https://www.fyeo.io/post/fyeo-3a-dao-security-assessment)

During the code review of the 3A Lending Protocol, FYEO discovered:

* 4 findings with a MEDIUM severity rating.
* 2 findings with a LOW severity rating

which have been addressed by 3A development team [Medium](https://blog.3adao.org/3a-lending-protocols-security-under-the-microscope-fyeo-audit-35de5a49e44b).
