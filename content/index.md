These are the docs for 3A DAO.

- **[swap.3adao.org](https://swap.3adao.org)**: buy OPSBTC and OPSSOL with USDC on Arbitrum, or with a token
  from another EVM chain, in one signature.
- **The 3A borrowing protocol**: EURO3, vaults, the Stability Pool and the A3A token.
